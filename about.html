About Fortune Jobs AI

Fortune Jobs AI is a free AI-powered job portal at fortunejobsai.com for college students, new graduates and job seekers. It lists 20,000+ open jobs refreshed daily from employer career boards, USAJOBS and job-search partners, and every listing links to the original posting.

Free tools include an ATS resume score, AI job matching against your resume, a visa sponsorship and OPT filter, an application tracker and a resume builder. Fortune Jobs AI Pro costs $9.99 a month for more results per search and more AI actions.

Fortune Jobs AI is operated by YOU DO LMS INC., a New Jersey corporation. Contact: info@fortunejobsai.com.
