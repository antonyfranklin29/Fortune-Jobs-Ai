Preserve the `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}` placeholder in both links. This is a transactional account message; newsletter consent and unsubscribe controls do not belong in it.

Changing the HTML and subject does not change the sender. Production custom SMTP was off when checked on 2026-10-18, so the default sender still appears as Supabase Auth. Once configured, send a test signup to a controlled inbox and verify sender, rendering, confirmation-link behavior, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and reply handling.
