# Fortune Jobs AI authentication email branding

`auth-email/confirm-signup.html` is the source for Supabase Authentication > Emails > Confirm sign up. Subject: `Confirm your Fortune Jobs AI account`. Preserve the `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}` placeholder in both links. This is a transactional account message; newsletter consent and unsubscribe controls do not belong in it.

The confirmation subject and HTML were saved in the production Supabase dashboard on 2026-10-18. Reloading the page showed the saved subject and branded preview. A live signup confirmation email has not yet been checked; previously sent emails cannot be changed retroactively.

Changing the HTML and subject alone does not change the sender. Custom SMTP is now enabled with the verified transactional sender described below. Never commit SMTP credentials. Before launch, test a fresh signup confirmation and its link in a controlled inbox, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC and reply handling. Apply a similar visual style to email-change and other active templates before launch.

On 2026-10-18, the Resend account was opened and `account.fortunejobsai.com` was created as a dedicated **sending-only** domain. Three records were saved in GoDaddy after Franklin completed its SMS verification: one DKIM TXT at `resend._domainkey.account` and two CNAMEs, `rsend.account` to `rsend.forge.rmta.net` and `send.account` to `send.forge.rmta.net`. GoDaddy reported success and its record count rose from 23 to 26. All three resolved from GoDaddy's authoritative nameserver and Google DNS. Resend later showed the domain as **Verified**. Existing website and Microsoft 365 records were not edited.

Franklin approved a Resend key with **Sending access** restricted to `account.fortunejobsai.com`. It was entered directly into Supabase, never committed or sent in chat. Production custom SMTP is enabled and persisted after reload: `no-reply@account.fortunejobsai.com`, sender name `Fortune Jobs AI`, host `smtp.resend.com`, port 465, username `resend`. Keep click tracking off for authentication links. The separate weekly newsletter remains disabled.

The first password-recovery test failed with SMTP `535 Authentication credentials invalid`: the UI copy accidentally included an accessibility field suffix after the key. The stored SMTP password was corrected to the exact key. A subsequent recovery request for the owned `info@fortunejobsai.com` account returned HTTP 200, and Resend recorded a message from `Fortune Jobs AI <no-reply@account.fortunejobsai.com>` with status **Delivered**. The reset link was not opened and no password was changed. Provider delivery does not by itself prove Outlook Inbox placement.

`auth-email/reset-password.html` is the source for the reset subject `Reset your Fortune Jobs AI password`. A delivered test exposed a template editing mistake: the original Supabase heading and introductory sentence had been left above the branded HTML, and the initial `<` of the doctype was missing. The production reset template was replaced with the complete source and verified to persist after reload. A second delivered test still showed the old content, apparently during template propagation. A third recovery request returned HTTP 200; Resend marked it **Delivered** and its preview showed only the clean Fortune Jobs AI design. No recovery link was opened or password changed.
