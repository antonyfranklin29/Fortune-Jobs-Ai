# Public catalogue health, 24 October 2026

The read-only check in `scripts/catalogue-health.mjs` pages through open public listings. It reports the number posted within 30 days, which of the 50 US states have a recent listing, and the newest `last_seen_at`. A daily GitHub Actions job runs the same check. It fails when there are fewer than 300 recent jobs, any US state has no recent listing, or no listing was seen in the last 72 hours. A failed run is visible in GitHub Actions; email notifications depend on the repository owner's GitHub notification settings. The check never invokes ingestion or incurs Adzuna API calls.

At the time of the initial check, the public catalogue had 756 open listings, 507 posted within 30 days across 35 US states plus Washington, DC, and the latest `last_seen_at` was 2026-10-17 20:23 UTC. The job therefore correctly reports `missing_recent_states` and `catalogue_not_refreshed`. These counts do not prove relevance for any role/state pair or that application links still work.

The read-only recheck on 2026-10-26 00:53 UTC still found 756 open listings: 540 legacy Dice, 137 legacy Indeed and 79 Adzuna. Of those, 494 were posted within 30 days, covering 35 states; 15 states had no recent listing. The latest `last_seen_at` remained 2026-10-17 20:23 UTC, about 196.5 hours old. No ingest was triggered during this check. The legacy source counts describe stored rows, not permission to refresh or republish those sources.

On October 25, the owner activated Adzuna API Access at £8/month (5,000 calls/month, 500/day, 2,000/week, 50/min). A three-call US IT feed run inserted 26 jobs. A 15-call Data Analyst pass targeting the 15 states without recent listings inserted 232 more. After those runs, the public check reported 1,014 open jobs, 750 posted within 30 days, recent listings in all 50 states, and a latest `last_seen_at` of 2026-10-26 02:09:08 UTC. The source mix was 540 legacy Dice, 137 legacy Indeed and 337 Adzuna. The worker ledger records 28 lifetime calls, and its temporary total cap of 100 leaves 72. The 15-call pass is marked `partial` because pages/cells remain; the dashboard Test panel showed a 500 after the database committed, so do not repeat it without checking the ledger first.

Coverage in all 50 states means at least one recent listing per state. It does not mean 100 jobs per role/state, that application links remain live, or that every state has comparable coverage. After this snapshot, the owner provided the Vault secret, `pg_net` was enabled, the temporary lifetime cap was removed, and the three-call hourly US IT feed schedule was activated. The first scheduled run still needs observation. Design a controlled US state rotation, followed by Canada, the UK and India where the provider permits. Do not treat legacy Dice/Indeed rows as permission to scrape or republish new listings.
