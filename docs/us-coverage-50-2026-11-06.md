# U.S. coverage target: 50 jobs per role per state — status and plan (2026-11-06)

Target (Franklin): at least 50 fresh, relevant jobs for every role in every U.S. state, plus DC. That is 26 roles × 51 regions = 1,326 cells.

## Where it stands (production, read-only, 2026-11-06 14:10 UTC)

- **Cells at 50 or more:** 62 of 1,326 (4.7%). The catalogue holds 9,201 open Adzuna jobs.
- **Best role:** Software Engineer reaches 50 in 21 states (average 55). Business Analyst reaches it in 9 and Data Analyst in 7.
- **Roles with no jobs:** Project Manager, Registered Nurse, Sales Representative, Product Manager, Marketing Manager, HR Manager, Customer Service Representative and Accountant have 0 everywhere. The IT feed never collects them, and the 24-hour boost excluded them.
- **Cells never tried:** 1,177 of the 1,264 cells below 50 have never been requested once.
- **Cells already searched in full:** 47 cells came back short of 50 after every page was fetched. Data Analyst in ND, WY and AK returned 0. In those states Adzuna has no more matching listings, so they cannot reach 50 from this source.

## What Codex started

On 2026-11-06 ~13:28 UTC Codex scheduled `fortune-us-coverage-51-next-pass`:
- it runs twice an hour, with 3 requests each;
- it covers 18 IT roles;
- it is set to expire on 2026-11-07 13:28 UTC.

Together with the hourly IT feed, that is about 216 Adzuna requests a day.

## The binding constraint: the Adzuna allowance

Adzuna's standard limits are 250 a day, 1,000 a week and 2,500 a month. The worker enforces these limits itself and stops when they run out.

- **Weekly allowance:** 618 of the 1,000 were used in the last 7 days. At 216 a day, the worker blocks itself in about 2 days.
- **Monthly allowance:** 2,500 a month sustains only about 83 requests a day.
- **Where the budget goes:** the hourly IT feed (72 a day) adds about 8 new jobs per request. A targeted role × state request adds about 25–40.

## Plan (ready, needs one SQL run in the dashboard)

The schedule change is `migrations/pending/20261106_coverage_50_all_roles.sql`; rollback is in the same file.

1. **Coverage job:** one role × state below 50 every 20 minutes, never-tried cells first, across all 26 roles. That is 72 requests a day.
2. **IT feed:** reduced to every 6 hours at 1 request (4 a day), so the newest IT listings keep flowing.
3. **Remove the 24-hour boost,** so the weekly allowance is not exhausted.

That totals about 76 requests a day: ~530 a week and ~2,300 per 30 days, inside the allowance. Every never-tried cell gets its first request within ~17 days. Cells with more pages stay due; cells whose market is exhausted are rechecked every 6 days by the worker.

## What 50-everywhere honestly needs

- **Large states and common roles** (Software Engineer, Business Analyst and Data Analyst in CA, TX, NY, IL, NC and similar) can reach 50 under this plan.
- **Small states and niche roles** (Anaplan, Tableau, Power BI, SQL Developer; ND, WY, AK, VT…) will not. Adzuna already returned every listing it has for several of them. No schedule change fixes that.
- **To go further:**
  - a higher Adzuna allowance (a partner agreement; Franklin's decision);
  - a second licensed job source;
  - showing nearby-state or remote jobs, clearly labelled, when a state is short. This is a product decision; listings are never padded silently.
