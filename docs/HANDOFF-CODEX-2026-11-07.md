# Handoff to Codex — 2026-11-07 (from Claude, for Franklin)

This is a status update covering what changed on Nov 6–7. All of it is live. **Nothing here needs you to deploy anything.** Please read it before working on job ingest, AI, billing or account code, so you build on the current version.

## 1. New job sources (live, scheduled)
| Source | Function | What it does | Rows in `pre_scraped_jobs` (Nov 7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company career boards | `ats-ingest` | Reads IT jobs from about 92 companies' own Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters boards. Boards are split into 4 slices by UTC hour, with concurrency 4. Links go only to the employer's page (aggregators are blocked). Descriptions are not copied (`job_description: null`). Unseen rows are closed per board. | greenhouse 723, ashby 197, lever 76, smartrecruiters 30 |
| USAJobs (official API) | `usajobs-ingest` | Federal IT and data jobs (series 2210, 1550, 1560, 0854, 1530). Links point to usajobs.gov only. Closed postings are skipped. Multi-city jobs list their real cities ("+ N more"). | usajobs 45 |

- **Secrets:** `USAJOBS_API_KEY` was added by Franklin. The User-Agent email is info@fortunejobsai.com.
- **Known limits:**
  - Remote-only jobs with no state are skipped, because `ingest_upsert_jobs` needs a state_code.
  - About 317 of 364 federal jobs are skipped because no role matches `ingest_classify_role`. An "IT Specialist" role was deferred by Franklin.
- **Careerjet was dropped**, because its Apply links did not reach employers. Branch `claude/careerjet-1107` is unused.
- **Code:** `supabase/functions/ats-ingest/`, `usajobs-ingest/`. Tests: `tests/ats-ingest.ts`, `tests/usajobs-ingest.ts`. `core.mjs` is generated by `scripts/build-ats-core.cjs`, and a parity test keeps it in sync.

## 4. Pro subscription via Stripe (built, live in TEST mode, ON HOLD for live)
- **Plan:** $9.99/month, up to 50 results per search and 50 AI Coach actions a day. Stripe price lookup key: `fortune_pro_monthly`.
- **Functions:**
  - `billing` handles checkout and the customer portal. It checks Stripe for an existing subscription so a person never gets a duplicate.
  - `billing-webhook` verifies signatures and deduplicates events through the `stripe_events` table. It handles `cancel_at`, and a cancelled duplicate never overwrites an active subscription.
  - `delete-account` cancels Stripe first, and aborts the deletion if Stripe refuses.
- **Database:** `subscriptions`, `is_pro()` and `stripe_events` (migration `migrations/pending/20261107a_subscriptions.sql`, already applied).
- **Test-mode gate:** the frontend has `PRO_OPEN=false` (`react/src/shared/billing.js`), and `?pro_test=1` reveals Pro for testing. The server allows test-mode checkout only for the owner account.
- **Why it's on hold:** the live restricted key needs verification by an admin of the Stripe account's email, and that person isn't available yet. Franklin said to hold.
  - Remaining steps once that's done: Franklin creates the live restricted key and replaces `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`. Then: a live webhook with the same 6 events, a live portal set to cancel at period end, the live `whsec`, one real $9.99 subscribe/cancel/refund test, then `PRO_OPEN=true` and removing the Terms "launching soon" wording.
  - **Please do not change billing keys, the webhook or `PRO_OPEN` without Franklin.**
- **Keys:** the Stripe account also processes another business's payments. The website key is least-privilege on purpose and cannot issue refunds. Refunds are done by Franklin in the Stripe dashboard.

## 5. Account emails (live)
- **Function:** `account-email`, sending from "Fortune Jobs AI <info@fortunejobsai.com>" via Resend.
- **Emails:** welcome, pro_started, pro_payment_failed, pro_cancelled, account_deleted. Each goes out only once, tracked in the `account_emails` table (unique on user, kind and key).
- **Welcome trigger:** `on_auth_user_confirmed_welcome` on `auth.users` (migration `20261107b_account_emails.sql`, applied).
- **Not yet tested end to end:** the Pro-cancelled email in live mode.

## 6. Terms (released today)
- **Refund policy:** the new section is at `terms.html#refunds`, modelled on LinkedIn, Jobscan and Teal:

  - Requests go to info@ with the subject "Refund Request", and we reply within 2 business days.
- **Pro card:** now links to the refund policy.
