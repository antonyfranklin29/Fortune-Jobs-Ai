### 2. Free tools for new grads

If you’re graduating soon, try this with one job you’d actually apply to: run a free ATS résumé check, then use AI matching to see how your experience lines up with the role.

Pick one relevant project, internship, or campus job and make your contribution clearer before you apply. A specific example gives a recruiter something to ask you about.

I built these tools into Fortune Jobs AI to make that first review easier.

https://fortunejobsai.com/resume-search.html

#NewGrad #ResumeTips #CareerAdvice

### 3. How to track applications

My rule for keeping up with applications: update the stage while the details are still fresh.

Save a role you want to revisit. Move it to Applied after submitting, Interview when one is scheduled, and Offer if you get one. Add a short note about the next step so you know what to do when you come back.

I built that workflow into Saved Jobs on Fortune Jobs AI.

https://fortunejobsai.com/profile.html
