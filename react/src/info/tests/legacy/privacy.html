Searching for jobs

Search terms and chosen location and filters are processed to find matching listings. Live Search sends those search settings to external job-search processing services. Searches have separate identifiers. Job listings are public information and matching does not guarantee employment or eligibility.

For eligible searches, Careerjet receives your search words, selected location, IP address and browser type to show live results and prevent fraud. We do not attach your name, email, résumé or account details to these requests. If you choose to search Jooble, we send Jooble your search words and selected location to show live listings.

Guest resume matching

Supported PDF and DOCX documents are read in your browser. The guest document is not uploaded to resume storage. Reduced role, skills, experience and home-area details are kept in this tab's temporary storage with a two-hour validity period. Expired details are discarded the next time the matching tools access them. Clear them with the Clear resume details option. Search links contain the job title, selected area and an opaque matching identifier. Avoid sharing those links if you want to keep your search private.

Résumé workspace and fit estimates

The résumé workspace formats details you enter in your browser tab. If you choose to review your saved profile, it reads your name, email, current title, confirmed skills and preferred location from your account; you decide whether to add them to the draft. If you select an existing text-based PDF or DOCX, the workspace reads the file locally and shows editable title and skill suggestions. The workspace does not save the draft or imported file to your account or send them to an AI provider. Closing or refreshing the tab discards the draft. If you print or save a PDF, that copy is controlled by your browser and device. Job-specific fit estimates compare confirmed résumé details with available listing text in your browser. They may be incomplete and are not hiring predictions.

AI features

Some features use an AI model provided by Anthropic (Claude) to write or explain content: job fit explanations, résumé tailoring, interview preparation, skill plans, plain-English search and employer job-post writing. They run only when you are signed in and choose an AI option. Depending on the feature, we send the job details shown, the résumé text saved in your profile, the role or search you typed, or, for employers, the job notes they type. We record which feature was used and how much text was processed, to apply daily limits and manage costs. We do not store the AI answers. Anthropic may keep request data for a limited time under its terms, for example to detect misuse. AI answers can be wrong, so review them before you rely on them.

Payments

If you subscribe to Pro, Stripe processes the payment and stores your card details under its own privacy policy. We receive and keep a Stripe customer ID, your subscription status, renewal date and whether you have cancelled, so we can turn Pro features on and off. We never receive your full card number.

Accounts and uploaded files

An account stores your sign-in email and the profile details you save, including your skills, experience, preferences, saved jobs and search history. Saving a resume to your profile stores the original file and extracted text. Resume files use private storage with temporary access links. Profile photos use public image URLs: anyone with the URL can view the photo.

Employer workspaces

Employer sign-in uses the same account identity as job-seeker sign-in, with a separate company workspace. We store company names, website domains, membership, job drafts and review history to verify companies and manage postings. Company details and job content become public only through approval. Permission evidence and review notes are kept private. Closing an account removes its membership and personal account references; company and job history may remain. A company without an active owner is paused or rejected, and reviewer-approved feeds may require another review.

Your controls

In your profile you can edit details, remove your resume and extracted text, remove saved jobs and clear search history. Download my data exports your stored profile, saved jobs, search history and email preferences; original files are not included. Store that download privately. Delete your account, at the bottom of your profile, permanently deletes your profile, extracted resume text, uploaded resume and photo files, saved jobs, search history and email preferences, then closes your account. You may be asked to sign in again first. Deleted data cannot be restored. Live Search usage records are kept without your account attached. If you are the last owner of an employer company, its live listings are closed.

Service providers and technical information

External services provide account authentication, cloud database and file storage, job-search processing, location suggestions and web assets. They may receive technical information such as IP address and request logs. Account sign-in uses browser storage to keep you signed in. Guest resume details use temporary tab storage. Resume text and account identity are not included in the live-search request.

Optional job alert emails

Creating an account or signing in with Google does not subscribe you to marketing emails. If you opt in on the email preferences page, we email new job listings that match the job title on your profile and your search area, daily or weekly as you choose, and skip the email when there are no new matches. Our email delivery provider (Resend) processes your email address, first name and the message content; your résumé is not sent to it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can change or stop alerts at any time on the email preferences page. Account and security messages are separate.

Retention and security

Guest matching details expire after two hours and are discarded on the next matching-tool access. Account data remains stored until changed or removed; there is no automatic account-data expiry schedule yet. Database access rules restrict private account records to their owner. We are still completing backup, recovery, abuse-prevention and security checks, and cannot promise uninterrupted service.
