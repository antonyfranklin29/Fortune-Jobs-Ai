Frequently asked questions

What is Fortune Jobs AI?

Fortune Jobs AI (fortunejobsai.com) is a free AI-powered job portal for college students, new graduates and job seekers. It lists 20,000+ open jobs refreshed daily, gives a free ATS resume score, and ranks jobs against your resume.

Is Fortune Jobs AI free?

Yes. Searching jobs, the ATS resume score, AI job matching and the application tracker are free. The free plan shows 10 results per search. Fortune Jobs AI Pro costs $9.99 a month for 50+ results per search and more AI actions.

Where do the jobs come from?

Jobs come from employer career boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Breezy), USAJOBS, and job-search partners such as Adzuna and WhatJobs. Every listing links to the original posting.

Can I find jobs that mention visa sponsorship or OPT?

Yes. A work-authorization filter shows jobs whose listings mention visa sponsorship or OPT, which helps international students. Always confirm sponsorship with the employer.

What kinds of jobs are listed?

Mostly tech and data roles (software engineer, data analyst, data engineer, business analyst), plus a growing number of healthcare jobs such as registered nurse, in all 50 states and Washington, DC.
