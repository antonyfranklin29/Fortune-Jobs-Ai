Free job application tracker

Fortune Jobs AI is a job portal with a free application tracker in your account. Save a job, apply through the original posting, and update its stage from Saved to Applied, Interview and Offer. You can also mark a rejection and keep private application notes. Stages are updated by you; the tracker does not submit applications or automatically know an employer’s decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is the application tracker free?

Yes. Create a free Fortune Jobs AI account to save jobs, update application stages and keep private notes.

What stages can I track?

Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer and Rejected are available. You update the stage as your application progresses.

Does it apply for me?

No. Use the original posting to apply. The tracker records the stage you choose and does not automatically confirm an employer’s decision.
